# Origin Island Pro v2.23.0 — Punch-Hole Camera Cutout & Alignment Tuner

### 🌟 What's New in v2.23.0

1. **Real-Time Punch-Hole Camera Alignment Tuner:**
   - **Interactive Live Preview Container:** Integrated a live simulated status bar island view with a real-time rendered camera hole lens (glossy dark blue radial gradient with lens bezel). As you adjust any setting, the preview reacts instantly at 60 FPS.
   - **Position Presets:** Seamless one-tap switching between **Center Cutout** (vivo X100, X200, iQOO 12, iQOO 13) and **Left Corner Cutout**.
   - **Continuous Camera Gap Width Slider:** Fine-tune camera hole clearance from **22 dp to 50 dp** with haptic feedback.
   - **Punch-Hole Safe Margin Slider:** Custom slider (**0 dp to 16 dp**) to control safe clearance between text/icons and the front camera lens, guaranteeing 0% text overlap on any device.

2. **One-Tap "Test on Status Bar 🚀" Live Injection:**
   - Tap the new status bar test button to cast a live alignment pill directly onto your phone's physical screen (`◄ L-Fit` and `R-Fit ►` with emerald badge and edge glow). Look up at your camera cutout to confirm pixel-perfect hardware alignment in seconds!

3. **In-App Settings UI Polish:**
   - Added clean Material 3 sliders, haptic ticks, and responsive preview layout in the new **Punch-hole & Camera Cutout** settings section.
