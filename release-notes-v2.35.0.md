## Origin Island Pro v2.35.0: vivo X200 / OriginOS 7

**New**
- Apps that already show their own vivo island are no longer cast twice ("Skip native islands", on by default).
- Long-press an entry in the Log tab to mute that app for 1 h or 8 h.
- ⋮ → Copy diagnostics: a one-paste device report.
- Delivery card shows vivo's native animated tick when the order is delivered.
- Chinese delivery, ride, payment and OTP text is understood (Bangladeshi and global apps unchanged).

**Fixes**
- A message like "missed video call" no longer becomes a call card.
- "Send"/"Extend" buttons were coloured red; buttons now use vivo's palette (red decline, green accept, neutral otherwise).
- 2FA prompts and Raiden now respect quiet hours.
- Cards stay on the island scene vivo actually grants, so they no longer risk being silently dropped.

**Look**
- Island: no invented glows or emoji; edge light only for payment success and charging.
- App: follows system light/dark mode and wallpaper colours, uses the system font.

Charging card is now off by default (vivo shows charging natively).
